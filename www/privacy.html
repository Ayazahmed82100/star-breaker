Star Breaker

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 8, 2026

Star Breaker ("the app") is a free arcade shooter game published by Aap ka naam ("I", "me", "the developer"). This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

1. Information I collect

I do not ask you to create an account, and I do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location data myself.

Your high score is stored only on your own device. It is not sent to me or to any server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, and how you interact with ads. This is done by Google, under Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in advertising

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Permissions

The app needs internet access to load ads. It does not need access to your camera, contacts, files or location.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact me so it can be removed.

5. Data sharing and security

I do not sell your personal information. The only third party involved is Google (AdMob), as described above. Because the app stores no personal data on my servers, there is no personal data held by me that could be exposed.

6. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date updated above.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact me at aap@gmail.com.
